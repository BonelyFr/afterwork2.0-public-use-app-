After Work – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

The short version

After Work does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your checklists, schedule edits, notes, workout plans and logs are saved locally on your device only. The app has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Backups

If you choose “Export backup” or “Share or save file”, the app creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, Messages, etc.) is entirely your choice. We never receive it.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new date.
